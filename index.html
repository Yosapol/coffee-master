A note about the wheel & our pairings

This is our own simplified discovery wheel, built for everyday coffee drinkers. The mood pairings are editorial choices: familiar cocoa for cozy moments, floral notes for a gentle pause, citrus for a bright moment, fruit for curiosity, caramel for focus, and spice for a bold mood. Your preferences always come first.

Flavor vocabulary is informed by the World Coffee Research Sensory Lexicon. For a professional tasting reference, explore the SCA Coffee Taster’s Flavor Wheel. Our wheel is an independent, simplified design.
