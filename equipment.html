First upgrade

Grinder

A consistent grinder improves sweetness, clarity, and repeatability more than most brewing accessories. Compare burr geometry, retention, alignment, noise, serviceability, and real brew results.

Daily control

Scale and timer

Measure dose, water, yield, and brew time. This makes recipes repeatable and troubleshooting possible.

Kettle and dripper

A steady kettle helps pouring. Dripper shape and filters change drawdown, body, bypass, and clarity.

Evaluation checklist

Use this before buying new gear.

ProblemWhat exact taste, speed, workflow, or capacity issue will this solve?
ConsistencyDoes it reduce variation between cups or create more variables?
MaintenanceCan staff clean it daily and find replacement parts locally?
TrainingCan a new barista use it correctly during a busy service?
DataCan you measure the improvement with brew time, yield, waste, or taste notes?
