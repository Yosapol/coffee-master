# Water card illustrations

Seven original illustrations generated with the built-in image generation tool. Transparent WebP files in this folder are used by water.html; original generation files are retained locally.

## Shared prompt

Use case: illustration-story. Asset type: small watercolor illustration for a Coffee Master water guidance card. [Subject below] Hand-painted artisanal watercolor, delicate pigment granulation and organic edges, muted sage green, ivory ceramic, dusty blush, pale blue grey, caramel. Cohesive with a premium watercolor coffee website. Square composition, objects fully visible and centrally grouped with generous breathing space. Genuinely transparent background, no paper rectangle, no border, no text, no letters, no logo, no watermark. Charming detailed household objects, not a flat vector icon or infographic.

## Subjects and final assets

- `clean-water.webp`: A clear household filter pitcher with a sage filter insert, partly filled with pale blue clear water, beside a small drinking glass.
- `minerals.webp`: A clear glass of water with a few small softly colored mineral crystal stones beside its base, subtle sage and lavender hues. No labels or scientific symbols.
- `balance.webp`: A small amber mineral-drop bottle with a glass pipette held just above a clear water carafe, one tiny droplet suspended, beside a sage coffee cup.
- `flat.webp`: Two small sage ceramic tasting cups of coffee side by side, a clear water glass behind them, inviting a comparison brew. Subtle muted coffee browns.
- `weak.webp`: A small clear glass server of very pale brewed coffee next to a modest scoop of ground coffee, pale caramel coffee color, delicate and understated.
- `harsh.webp`: A small warm terracotta coffee cup beside a simple slim unmarked thermometer and a few gentle wisps of steam, no numbers. Soft muted rose accent.
- `scale.webp`: An open sage gooseneck kettle viewed from above at an angle so pale chalky mineral deposits along its inner bottom are visible, lid resting beside it. Clearly a kettle with a long curved spout.
