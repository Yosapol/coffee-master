# Equipment card artwork

Created with the built-in image generation tool; used as transparent 480px WebP assets in this folder. Original generation files are retained locally. The kettle and dripper card reuses assets/watercolor-brew.webp.

Shared prompt: Small square watercolor illustration for a premium home coffee website. [Subject] Delicate hand-painted pigment granulation, muted sage, blush, ivory, caramel palette. Objects fully visible, centrally grouped, generous breathing space, transparent background, no paper rectangle. No text, logos, letters, numbers or watermark. Cohesive with an artisanal watercolor coffee collection.

- grinder.webp: A compact sage electric burr coffee grinder with a clear hopper of roasted beans and a small grounds cup beside it.
- scale.webp: A small sage digital coffee scale with a plain unmarked display, a ceramic cup on its platform, and a little analog timer beside it.
- espresso.webp: A compact home espresso machine in soft sage and brushed warm silver, a portafilter fitted in its group head, two ivory espresso cups and a little milk pitcher.
